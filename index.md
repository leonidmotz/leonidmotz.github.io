# Leonid Motz

I am a historical linguist interested in Slavic contact linguistics, how languages change, and why, when and to what extent people start writing down the way they speak (do they ever, really?). Core research foci include the internal and external history of Balkan Slavic (specifically in the realm of its morphology), Wallachian Chancellery Slavic, and digital methods in Philology. Currently, I am a Predoctoral Researcher in Digital Humanities at the University of Vienna's [Department of Linguistics](https://ufind.univie.ac.at/de/person.html?id=1005821), working within the [Cluster of Excellence »EurAsian Transformations«](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/eurasian-transformations/people/affiliated-members/leonid-motz). This website is a space for some of my personal projects.

If you want to contact me, please write to [info@leonidmotz.eu](mailto:info@leonidmotz.eu).

## Projects

### Helpers, Tools and Indices
- [Wlachtype](/wlachtype/): A virtual keyboard for transcribing Wallachian Cyrillic. Expandable for other glyphs, if needed. Requires a Russian keyboard to be used 
- [Derksen Index](/derksen-index/): An interactive lemma index for Rick Derksen's Etymological Dictionary of the Slavic inherited lexicon, linking lemma to page.

### Datasets and Corpora
- [Case Analogy in Codex Suprasliensis](https://phaidra.univie.ac.at/detail/o:2343117): TOROT/PROIEL data augmented by a layer of historical nominal morphology (stem class, analogy status), permitting the description and analysis of the transformation of the inflectional system. Part of my MA thesis.
